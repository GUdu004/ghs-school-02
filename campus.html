Physical Security • Maternal Reassurance

A Sanctuary for Learning.

Physical safety is the foundation of intellectual and spiritual flourishing. Guided Hearts Schools is nestled inside the protected enclave of Peace Haven Gated Estate with a dedicated, traffic-free walking corridor to the local central mosque.

Location Architecture

The 7-Meter Mosque Corridor

When evaluating a faith-based school, mothers rightly ask: How do our children get to the mosque for congregational prayers? Are they exposed to road traffic?

At Guided Hearts Schools, our campus is positioned just 7 meters from the neighborhood central mosque entirely within the gated estate perimeter. Pupils do not cross public highways or commercial streets.

During daily Dhuhr prayers, pupils walk in calm, escorted lines accompanied by teachers and caregivers, fostering reverence, safety, and community camaraderie.

Campus Facilities & Security Protocols

Clean, ventilated, hygienic, and secure spaces designed to support attentive scholarship and energetic childhood play.

Manned Estate Perimeter

Peace Haven Estate operates a 24/7 security gatehouse with strict visitor log-in verification, license plate tracking, and active barrier gates.

Community Mosque Sanctuary

Serene, carpeted prayer hall where pupils observe daily congregational Dhuhr prayer and Tarbiyyah circles alongside their teachers.

Modern Ventilated Classrooms

Sunlit, ergonomic learning spaces equipped with educational wall charts, individualized wooden desks, and dedicated teacher mentorship.

Operational Discipline

Child Pick-Up & Guardian Verification Protocol

No pupil is released to any individual without a pre-registered parental authorization card. In the event of emergency pickup changes, parents must provide verifiable confirmation directly through our administrative line before gate security clears release.
