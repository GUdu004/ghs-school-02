Institutional Governance • Child Welfare

Safeguarding & Privacy Code.

In an institution dedicated to spiritual uprightness, the physical, psychological, and digital protection of every child is a sacred trust (Amanah).

1. Child Protection & Anti-Maltreatment Mandate

Guided Hearts Schools maintains an absolute Zero-Tolerance Policy toward any form of physical abuse, emotional humiliation, harsh corporal punishment, or neglect. Every child under our care is entitled to an environment defined by gentleness, dignity, and active protection.

  • Staff Background Vetting: All instructional, administrative, and maintenance staff undergo thorough identity verification, criminal background checks, and professional reference reviews prior to contact with pupils.
  • Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL): Our governing board maintains an independent safeguarding lead responsible for investigating any student concern with complete confidentiality.
  • Open Reporting Channels: Pupils are taught safe personal boundaries and are provided accessible, pressure-free avenues to express any distress to trusted counselors.

2. Student Media & Photographic Consent Policy

We respect the privacy of our pupils and their families in an increasingly connected digital world. In compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) and international privacy best practices:

  • Explicit Parental Consent: Photographs, project videos, or academic work featuring identifiable students are never published on our website or promotional media without signed written consent from a parent or legal guardian.
  • Metadata Sanitization: All digital assets published on this platform have geographical coordinates (GPS EXIF tags) and camera hardware metadata permanently stripped to prevent location tracking.
  • Right to Revocation: Parents reserve the unconditional right to withdraw digital media consent at any point by contacting our administration in writing.

3. Digital Information Privacy & Data Handling

Information submitted via our digital admissions forms or WhatsApp communication channels—including birth records, contact details, and academic evaluations—is stored on encrypted, access-restricted institutional systems. We do not sell, rent, or share prospective parent data with third-party advertising brokers under any circumstances.

Safeguarding Inquiries & Confidential Reports

For any concerns regarding child welfare or data privacy, please contact our Board Governance Committee directly at governance@guidedhearts.sch.ng.
