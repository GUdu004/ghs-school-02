Academic Structure • Session 2026/2027

The Mastery of Two Worlds.

We reject the false dichotomy that faith and intellectual inquiry are at odds. Our dual-curriculum architecture provides pupils with either an immersive classical Islamic foundation or a seamless hybrid of national academic standards and Tahfeez.

Track A • Hybrid Western & Islamic

Hybrid Academic Track

Designed for prospective doctors, engineers, economists, and scholars. Balances standardized national examinations with Quranic fluency and moral uprightness.

Standardized Examinations Prepares for State & National Entrance Examinations (BECE/Transition)

Core Subject Disciplines

  • STEM & Mathematics: Algebra, Geometry, Applied Arithmetic & Problem Solving
  • Science & Tech: Physics, Chemistry, Biology Foundations & ICT Coding
  • Literacy & Humanities: Advanced English Grammar, Creative Writing & Literature
  • Tahfeez & Tajweed: Memorization of Juzu' Amma & Tabarak with verified rules
  • Arabic Language: Conversational Arabic, Vocabulary & Script Writing
  • Tarbiyyah & Hadith: Daily Adhkar, 40 Hadith of An-Nawawi, Akhlaq mentorship

Track B • Classical Depth

Full Islamic Scholarship Track

An intensive immersive track designed for families seeking complete Quranic memorization, deep classical Arabic syntax, and foundational Islamic jurisprudence.

Practical Scholarship

Scientific Inquiry Rooted in Purpose.

In our classrooms and laboratories, science is not taught as mere abstract formulas. Pupils engage in experiential learning—observing botanical processes, exploring optics and basic circuit mechanics, and conducting controlled chemistry experiments.

Under the pedagogy leadership of Brother Adeniyi Ibrahim (13+ years consulting), our teachers employ active-learning strategies that foster critical reasoning, inquisitive questioning, and mathematical precision, ensuring pupils meet the highest national examination thresholds.
