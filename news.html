COMMUNITY MOSQUE PARTNERSHIP September 2026

Joint Friday Jumu'ah Mentorship Circle Inaugurated

In partnership with the neighborhood Central Mosque committee, Guided Hearts pupils in upper classes participate in weekly escorted Friday reflections and Tajweed circles following Jumu'ah prayer, reinforcing communal brotherhood and practical Islamic etiquette.

Term Dates & Islamic Calendar Synchronization

Our academic timetable harmonizes national term milestones with the Hijri calendar, ensuring dedicated observances for Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, and Eid al-Adha without compromising the required instructional hours in national curriculum subjects.
