Tarbiyyah & Culture

A Day of Purpose and Grace.

At Guided Hearts, spiritual uprightness is not an isolated lecture; it is the lived experience of every hour. We structure our days to nurture inquisitive intellects, disciplined habits, and compassionate hearts.

Daily Schedule

The Daily Rhythm of a Guided Hearts Pupil

A carefully calibrated balance of worship, deep academic work, energetic play, and ethical mentorship.

07:45 • Arrival & Morning Adhkar

A Peaceful Beginning

Pupils are greeted warmly at our gated entry, place their materials in lockers, and gather for morning remembrances (Adhkar), supplication, and brief moral reflections.

08:15 • Academic & STEM Block

Analytical Discovery

High-focus morning sessions covering Mathematics, English literacy, foundational sciences, and computing skills taught by qualified specialist educators.

10:30 • Mid-Morning Break & Play

Social Recreation

Supervised outdoor play in our enclosed courtyard, wholesome snacks, and social interaction under watchful teacher guidance.

11:00 • Quranic Tahfeez & Arabic Immersion

Connecting with the Revelation

Small recitation circles (Halaqat) where pupils memorize, revise, and refine pronunciation (Tajweed) and conversational Arabic according to their individualized learning pace.

13:00 • Wudu & Congregational Dhuhr Prayer

Worship in the Central Mosque

Pupils perform ablution with care and walk via our 7m protected estate corridor to join the congregation at the neighborhood central mosque, learning the sanctity of communal prayer.

13:45 • Lunch & Adab Circle

Character & Table Manners

Wholesome lunch accompanied by lessons in gratitude, mindful eating, table etiquettes, and short stories from the Seerah and 40 Hadith of Imam An-Nawawi.

15:30 • Dismissal & Verified Pickup

Secure Departure

Closing supplications and organized departure adhering strictly to our authorized guardian identification protocol.

Moral Architecture

Zero Tolerance for Bullying. Deep Cultivation of Adab.

We believe a child cannot learn effectively if they feel threatened or marginalized. Guided Hearts Schools enforces an unyielding anti-bullying policy rooted in the Islamic principle that every believer is a brother to another.

Teachers actively model and reward gentleness, patience, humility, and respectful speech. Disagreements are resolved through guided mediation and Islamic principles of mutual forgiveness and accountability.
